Earth

A small blue planet, and the only place we know that is home to life. Scroll to take a closer look.

Third planet from the Sun.

Water

Oceans cover about 71% of the surface and hold 97% of all water. Seen from space, they give our planet its color.

Average ocean depth: 3,700 m.

Air

A thin atmosphere, only a few dozen kilometres deep, shields life from radiation and keeps the climate steady.

Nitrogen 78%, oxygen 21%.

Land

Seven continents rest on tectonic plates that drift a few centimetres every year, slowly reshaping the map.

Highest peak: Everest, 8,849 m.

Life

Millions of species live here, from microbes in deep rock to whales in open water. Life has been present for more than 3.5 billion years.

About 8.7 million species, by one estimate.

Motion

Earth spins once a day and travels around the Sun once a year, at roughly 30 km every second. Its tilt of 23.4 degrees gives us the seasons.

Day: 24 hours. Year: 365.25 days.

Home

4.5 billion years old. 12,742 km across. One moon, one home.
